The business advantage of coops

I mean, is it just me, or does it seem absurd that every time someone wants to host a software as a service, they have to reinvent an SRE organization?
A co-op could offer reduced rates to members, higher standards and transparency to customers and long term stability because the business model is driven by customer success instead of transforming value to cash for shareholders.
As a developer, if your service comes to depend on a provider who is controlled by shareholder expectations, then you risk disruption when they inevitably get acquired.

Instead, if all of your providers are aligned with long term sucess and continuous evolution, then everyone benefits from stability and shared understanding.
(ACME and Let's Encrypt for example)

The technical advantage
IME, the terms SRE and DevOps are aligned with organizations and infrastructure. They are still biased toward local optimization.
