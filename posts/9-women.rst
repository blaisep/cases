How to hire 9 women to have a baby in a month
==============================================

Venture funded startups get forced into a situation that they only know how to survive by hiring nine women to have a baby in a month.
They keep trying this approach because occasionally it works.
They don't understand the reason behind the sporadic success.
The secret is that one of the women is eight months pregnant.
