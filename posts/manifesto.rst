Mission
Develop sustainable business to create and retain value through research, development and continuous improvement.
Focus on disruptive innovation to exploit the systemic weaknesses in the processes of organizations built around extractive principles.
Pursue continous improvement in operational practices to put more opportunities within reach without depending on external financing.
Achieve sustainability through diversity of business models, leveraging a common base in operational infrastructure.
Maintain financial independence by increasing revenues, or selling businesses which have appreciated in value.



Observations

These observations made over the past three decades support several conclusions. Some of these observations should elicit curiosity and deeper investigation. Each has supportive material in separate docs.

* Technology companies evolve along a predictable lifecycle which is susceptible to disruption.
* The financial structure of companies prevents them from responding properly to disruptive competition. These are systemic weaknesses.
* An organization can anticipate the systemic weaknesses and make informed decisions about how to proceed.
* This strategy is not generally available in management practices because it requires cross-disciplinary collaboration.
* One of the charachteristics of these systemic weaknesses is the difficulty in accounting for the value of human capital.
* Another charachteristic is the difficulty accounting for "technical debt" as a liability in the balance sheet.
* Companies with extensive debt to external shareholders will pass up opportunities
* Corporations with large stakeholder ownership tend to be more resilient and successful throughout the ups and downs of business cycles.
* A successful corporation depends on a wide range of high-quality expertise is disparate disciplines. This dependency represents a barrier to entry for many communities seeking to build sustainable businesses.

Proposal
I propose that we create a business with a high degree of stakeholder ownership, a low-dependence on short-term capital and a rigoruous committment to continuous improvement in operational methods.
