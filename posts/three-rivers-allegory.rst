The three rivers pattern
========================

What made the Bay Area unusual was the confluence of three societies:

    - Agriculture/Manufacturing
    - Research/Development
    - Business services

Each society had its own set of incentives, constraints and the culture surrounding them. Of the three, services has the weakest link to outcomes. In fact, services were typically paid for activity and there was no incentive to optimize from from one project to another.

By the 90's, Agriculture and Manufacturing were nearly extinct. A decade later, with the end of the cold war, the last of the  defense, pharma and semiconductor research giants consolidated or faded away.
Services became information services and colloquially called "Tech". But fundamentally, they were still advertising, financial, entertainment companies.
In the very beginning of rare few of these (Google, Netflix) had strong R&D contingents (NASA, SRI, Sun Micro)
