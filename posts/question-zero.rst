Question Zero
=============

This is a nod toward the four golden signals of Monitoring (Latency , Traffic, Errors, Saturation)

The four enduring mysteries
---------------------------

There are four questions which stakeholders often ask and developers struggle to answer:
(Success) How can I tell if it’s working?
(Failure) What happens when something goes wrong?
(Progress) How much better is the new version than the previous?
(Usage) How can I tell if anyone is using the feature?

This is assuming that the organization cares about success and does not conflate activity with progress.
To assess where the org is, I would suggest "Question 0", which is actually a variant of question 3

How would you describe how much better you are today than when you started?

I suggest that there are three possible answers, in order of frequency.
	1- We don't know and it's not important.
	2- We would love to know and we're not sure how to measure it.
	3- This is what we have tried in the past and this is how we're currently approaching the question.

four golden signals of Monitoring
-
    Latency Latency is the time taken to serve a request, widely known as response time. ...
- Traffic Traffic is the number of requests flowing across your network. ...
- Errors Errors indicate the rate of requests that fail. ...
- Saturation Saturation is the overall capacity of your services. ...
